# Chapter 15 – Working with PDF and Word Documents 📄📝

## Topics Covered
- Reading PDF files and extracting text  
- Merging and splitting PDF documents  
- Encrypting and decrypting PDFs  
- Creating and editing Word documents with python-docx  
- Reading text from Word files  
- Working with paragraphs and runs  
- Converting Word documents to PDF using Word automation  

## Goal
Automate repetitive tasks involving documents, such as extracting text, generating reports, converting formats, and handling large collections of PDF or Word files.

## Notes
Document automation is widely used in real workflows: generating invoices, processing reports, converting files, and extracting information from documents without manual work.

## Projects
pdf_paranoia_encrypt.py (encrypts PDFs and saves a copy with the suffix _encrypted.pdf.)

pdf_paranoia_decrypt.py (Finds encrypted PDFs, attempts to decrypt them and creates a decrypted copy.)

custom_invitations.py (Generate a Word document with one invitation per guest name.)

brute_force_pdf_password.py (Try passwords from a dictionary file to unlock an encrypted PDF.)
